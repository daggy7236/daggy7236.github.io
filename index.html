Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you book

Which areas of Cape Town do you service?

BITO ELECTRICAL serves Sea Point, Milnerton, Table View, Goodwood, Century City, Montague Gardens, Parklands, Blouberg and surrounding Cape Town areas. Contact us with your suburb to confirm availability.

Can I request an electrical COC?

Yes. BITO ELECTRICAL provides electrical inspections and Certificate of Compliance services. The installation must meet applicable requirements before a certificate can be issued.

Do you install solar, inverters and batteries?

Yes. Solar installation, solar maintenance, inverter systems, batteries and backup power solutions are available.

Do you install gate motors, CCTV and access control?

Yes. Security and automation services include CCTV, intercoms, electric fencing, gate motors, garage door motors and selected access-control and automation systems.

Do you provide electrical work for events and exhibition stands?

Yes. BITO ELECTRICAL has experience wiring exhibition stands and event installations and can discuss electrical requirements for temporary event or exhibition setups in Cape Town.
